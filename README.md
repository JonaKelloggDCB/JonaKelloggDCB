### Jonathan Kellogg

DPhil candidate in Politics at Oxford (Balliol). I work on the political economy of advanced democracies, European policy and legislative bargaining, and I build tools to measure things that are hard to measure.

**Projects**

- [yaab27.eu](https://yaab27.eu): a public ideas platform for the Young Academic Advisory Board of Democracy27, where people across the EU share one change they would make to restore confidence in democracy.
- [Pin the Headline](https://jonakelloggdcb.github.io/pin-the-headline/): a game about reading political approval curves, with 100+ leaders across 25 countries, plus a [US presidents edition](https://jonakelloggdcb.github.io/pin-the-presidents/).
- [Frankenstein's Bills](https://github.com/JonaKelloggDCB/Frankenstein-s-Bill): how legislators bundle low-visibility provisions into larger bills, in France, the US and the EU.

More at [jonakelloggdcb.github.io/academic-website](https://jonakelloggdcb.github.io/academic-website/).
